The visitor destination in Cornwall is nestled in a huge crater. Here, massive Biomes housing the largest rainforest in captivity, stunning plants, exhibitions and stories serve as a backdrop to some striking contemporary gardens, summer concerts and exciting year-round family events.

![eden-project-cornwall](https://res.cloudinary.com/dpgowkqsr/image/upload/v1611944649/70_FF_1627_9_ACC_486_F_82_EF_80_E81_B598274_15fb9bf91e.jpg)

Here it is, the Eden Project! You’ll find here lots of great topics like sustainability and supporting local communities. I am really curious to see what has changed/ been added so we may pay Eden another visit!
